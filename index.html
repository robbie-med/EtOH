Before you use this tool

This tool is for licensed healthcare professionals. It is not for patient self-assessment, and not for patients or caregivers to make treatment decisions.

Decision support only. It does not replace clinical judgement, examination, or your local protocol. Verify every dose against your own formulary and institutional guideline before administration.

Scoring instruments are reproduced from their published sources and linked in-app. Some treatment thresholds and dosing tiers come from named protocols rather than from the instrument itself, and are labelled where that is the case. Guidelines change — check the version and review status under About.
